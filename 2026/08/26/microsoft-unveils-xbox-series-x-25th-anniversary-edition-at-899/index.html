Microsoft Unveils Xbox Series X 25th Anniversary Edition at $899

By Justus
August 26, 2026 1 Min Read
0

Microsoft officially announced the Xbox Series X 25th Anniversary Edition on August 26, 2026, priced at $899 (approximately RMB 6,057), with pre-orders opening immediately. The console features 1TB of storage and adopts the iconic semi-transparent “OG Green” design from the original Xbox, bundled with a download code for Halo: Combat Evolved. Pre-orders begin at 22:00 Beijing Time on August 27, with the official release set for November 13. The accompanying X25 wireless controller is also available for pre-order at $79.99. This launch commemorates the 25th anniversary of the Xbox brand, following its initial debut at the Xbox Games Showcase in June.
