Dongfeng Motor Kicks Off 10th Technology Innovation Week and First User Lifestyle Festival in Wuhan

September 14, 2026 Last updated : September 14, 2026 Justus

On September 14, the opening ceremony of Dongfeng Motor's 10th Technology Innovation Week and First User Lifestyle Festival, themed "Eastern Wind Rises, Intelligently Painting a New Life," was held in Wuhan. At the event, Dongfeng Motor officially unveiled the "Sky Clean Zero Carbon" plan, the Embodied Intelligent Vehicle All-Domain Guardian Plan, and the "Sky Horizon Sail" plan, showcasing the company's latest technological achievements in digital intelligence energy, all-domain safety, and global layout. The company also officially launched the "Dongfeng Youxin" user service brand, built on four core pillars—Commitment, Trust, Promise, and Value—to establish direct user connections and accelerate co-creation through dedicated channels and platforms, fulfilling whole-lifecycle service commitments.

Yang Qing, Chairman and Party Secretary of Dongfeng Motor, attended the opening ceremony and delivered remarks. He stated that Dongfeng Motor focuses on the needs of safe, intelligent, and green mobility, continuously unlocking new experiences in smart products, and has earned the trust and recognition of over 64 million users. Looking toward the 15th Five-Year Plan period, Dongfeng Motor will fully embrace the wave of the AI era, deepen its engagement in green and low-carbon industries, and concentrate on national strategic needs, supply chain security and controllability, user demand cultivation, and product competitiveness enhancement—delivering products and services that exceed expectations and create a better automotive life for users.

Three Major Announcements Chart a New Course for Leapfrog Development

In response to the national dual-carbon strategy, Feng Changjun, General Manager and Deputy Party Secretary of Dongfeng Motor, officially released the "Sky Clean Zero Carbon" plan. He stated that the convergence of societal, user, and industrial evolution forms the foundational vision behind this initiative, ultimately crystallizing into a "1331" strategic framework: One vision—making user travel more effortless, industrial ecosystems more harmonious, and the social environment greener. Three core values—for users, making charging as convenient as refueling and turning vehicles into appreciating assets; for industry, partnering with ecosystem allies to aggregate over 10 million sets of charging infrastructure covering all user travel scenarios; for society, achieving annual collaborative carbon reduction of over 1 million tons, equivalent to planting 50 million trees each year. Three pillars—centered on green, convenience, and symbiosis, leveraging intelligent electric vehicles, new mobility services, and new energy ecosystems to reshape a harmonious industrial paradigm. One brain—precisely aggregating and intelligently orchestrating the three pillars.

At the opening ceremony, the Embodied Intelligent Vehicle All-Domain Guardian Plan was officially released. You Zheng, Vice President and Standing Committee Member of Dongfeng Motor, stated that under the wave of intelligentization, vehicle safety has upgraded from single-point protection to a multi-dimensional collaborative all-domain safety paradigm. Dongfeng Motor will create a deployable, perceivable, and trustworthy all-domain safety guardian solution. With value propositions of "peace of mind, peace at home, safe travel, and safe return," anchored to a "six zeros" vision—zero casualties, zero fires, zero hazards, zero accidents, zero leaks, and zero losses—the plan builds a "1+6+4" all-domain safety technology system: one embodied intelligence technology foundation, six major safety technology domains, and four industrial collaborative ecosystems.

To accelerate the development pattern of "in the world, for the world," Zhou Feng, Vice President and Standing Committee Member of Dongfeng Motor, released the "Sky Horizon Sail" plan. He stated that this plan will reshape Dongfeng's globalization path, bringing its intelligent and green mobility services to global customers and practicing "New Auto, New Dongfeng" through action. The plan anchors a "125" layout: "1" core foundation—China, strengthening the global intelligent manufacturing and export hub; "2" strategic highlands—passenger vehicles focusing on Europe and the Americas, commercial vehicles deeply cultivating the Americas and Central-North Asia, engaging in localized operations amid fierce global competition; "5" key pivots—Southeast Asia, Central-North Asia, the Middle East, North-Central Africa, and Australia-New Zealand, implementing differentiated and precisely targeted resource investment.

Deepening Ecosystem Collaboration with Multiple New Partnerships

At the opening ceremony, Dongfeng Motor deepened ecosystem collaboration with multiple new partnerships accelerating into place. At the level of technical standards co-construction, Dongfeng joined central enterprises, industry institutions, research institutes, and universities to jointly release the "White Paper on All-Domain Vehicle Safety in the Embodied Intelligence Era," aiming to redefine the conceptual boundaries of all-domain vehicle safety in the intelligent era based on embodied intelligent vehicle trends, outputting referenceable and deployable technical standards, and providing important references for industry safety technology evolution.

Two major research platforms were officially established at the event. The Dongfeng Motor Branch of the State Key Laboratory of Intelligent Green Vehicles and Transportation was unveiled, with both parties focusing on joint research into automotive embodied intelligence technology. The Dongfeng Motor Basic and Advanced Technology Research Institute was also officially inaugurated, reinforcing foundational research and advanced technology layout to solidify the foundation for Dongfeng's long-term technological competitiveness. Meanwhile, Dongfeng Motor signed a strategic cooperation agreement with Wuhan University of Technology, focusing on next-generation energy systems, advanced body and chassis technologies, and other core directions to create a benchmark for industry-academia-research collaborative innovation between central enterprises and universities.

Technology Extravaganza Makes Advanced Tech Tangible

From core three-electric systems to smart platforms, from key chips to complete vehicle matrices, this 10th Technology Innovation Week not only gathered Dongfeng Motor's full-chain hardcore technology exhibits but also showcased new models and the latest technological achievements. Through open immersive tours, real-vehicle test drives, and other rich experiences, Dongfeng's technology is made visible and touchable.

Exhibits including the energy ecosystem sandbox, Quantum Architecture 4.0, and 12C ultra-fast charging battery cells were showcased, systematically presenting Dongfeng's technological foundation in new energy, intelligent connectivity, and intelligent driving. The brand exhibition area assembled multiple heavyweight models including the M-Hero M817, Yijing X9, and Dongfeng Aeolus L8+, alongside commercial vehicle new products such as pure electric tractors, hydrogen fuel cell tractors, and solid-state battery vans, covering diverse scenarios from off-road to family to business use.

On September 13, at the First User Lifestyle Festival, Dongfeng Motor officially launched the "Dongfeng Youxin" user service brand. The brand carries four core pillars—Commitment, Trust, Promise, and Value—connecting with users through standardized car purchasing experiences, personalized test drive services, warm delivery experiences, "111" service standards, and direct user voice channels, fulfilling whole-lifecycle service commitments. It also introduced two major user benefits—the "User Return Gift" and "User Renewal Gift"—translating commitments to users into tangible rights and benefits, embodying the warmth and responsibility of a central enterprise automaker.

Additionally, a deep test drive and ride experience zone was set up at the Longling Mountain test track, offering multi-scenario intelligent driving experiences and immersive off-road test drives, inviting users to personally experience the real performance of "Made by Dongfeng." Simultaneously, in dual-line collaboration with the German Hannover Motor Show, overseas partners and influencers were invited to participate deeply in the experience, conveying Dongfeng Motor's global technological and product strength.

Eastern wind rises, intelligently painting a new life. In the future, Dongfeng Motor will consistently uphold high-level technological self-reliance, solidify its full-stack technology foundation, amplify the synergy of its open and collaborative ecosystem, and accelerate toward becoming a technology-driven, international, and youth-oriented Dongfeng. The company will transform technological innovation into product and service value that users can perceive, steadily advancing toward its established 2030 goals, and contributing solid Dongfeng strength to accelerating the building of an automotive powerhouse and elevating Chinese automotive brands.
